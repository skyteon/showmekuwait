The Opportunity

Kuwait has built the gateway. Now the world needs the story.

Coastline, heritage, gastronomy, desert landscapes and a deep tradition of hospitality. Kuwait has the raw material of a memorable destination, and with Visit Kuwait it now has a national platform to welcome visitors. The next step is reaching travelers who have not yet imagined Kuwait as a place to go.

01

The Infrastructure Is in Place

The Visit Kuwait platform brings e-visas, itineraries, events and offers together under one national brand, and the new terminal at Kuwait International Airport will expand capacity. Kuwait is ready to receive more international visitors.

02

Travelers Choose With Their Hearts First

A destination is chosen emotionally long before it is planned. For many travelers in Europe, Asia and the Americas, Kuwait does not yet have a picture attached to its name. Cinematic storytelling creates that picture.

03

Stories Travel Further Through People

Independent films and trusted creators reach audiences that official channels alone rarely reach. Visit Saudi's creator program, with more than 300 creators and over 240 million people reached, showed how quickly perception can shift.

04

Vision 2035 Invites Partnership

Kuwait's national development plan identifies tourism as a pillar of diversification and calls for private-sector partners in media and content. Show Me Kuwait is designed to be one of those partners.

How Show Me Kuwait fits

Show Me Kuwait does not replace the national platform. It works in front of it, bringing new international audiences into the funnel that Visit Kuwait already serves.

The Program

What Show Me Kuwait delivers

Not a single campaign, but a long-term content program with four parts that work together and point travelers to Kuwait's national gateway.

◎

Cinematic Destination Films

Flagship films and short series on Kuwait's coastline, heritage, food and people, made to the standard that has won awards at international tourism film festivals.

◉

International Creator Program

Travel creators from Europe, Asia, the Americas and the Middle East experience Kuwait firsthand and share it with their own audiences, alongside Kuwaiti creators.

◈

Social Media Channel

A dedicated @showmekuwait presence publishing regular English-language content, building a global community that sees Kuwait as a place worth discovering.

◆

Inspiration Guides

English editorial guides on showmekuwait.com that answer what international travelers search for, and link them to Visit Kuwait for visas, bookings and events.

From Vision to Execution

How we bring it to life

A phased plan that starts with a single flagship film and grows into a sustained international presence, developed together with Kuwait's tourism stakeholders.

Phase 01

Discover

Time on the ground in Kuwait with the tourism team and local partners: its neighborhoods, coastline, islands, desert, cuisine and people. We define the story Kuwait wants to tell and the international audiences it wants to reach.

Phase 02

Flagship Film

A cinematic Show Me Kuwait film that captures the country's character in a few minutes, made to travel online and to compete at international tourism film festivals.

Phase 03

Content Library

Short-form videos, photography, location stories and English guides on gastronomy, heritage and experiences. A library that Visit Kuwait, airlines and hotels can also use in their own channels.

Phase 04

Creator Program

International travel creators are hosted in Kuwait in planned journeys with hotel and airline partners, producing authentic content for audiences in their home markets.

Phase 05

Distribution

Consistent publishing on @showmekuwait and showmekuwait.com, festival submissions, media partnerships and presence alongside Kuwait at international tourism fairs. Every piece points travelers to Visit Kuwait.

Phase 06

Measure & Expand

Regular reports on reach, engagement and traffic sent to Visit Kuwait, followed by seasonal campaigns, themed series and event coverage that keep Kuwait in front of international travelers year after year.
